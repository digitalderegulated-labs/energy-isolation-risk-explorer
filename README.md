## Overview

This dashboard analyzes severe workplace incidents across infrastructure-heavy industries to identify patterns in **energy isolation risk**.

It focuses on environments with complex electrical systems — including power generation, data centers, telecommunications networks, and commercial infrastructure — where multiple active energy sources increase operational risk during maintenance.

The objective is to transform raw OSHA incident data into **clear, decision-ready intelligence** for understanding where isolation failures may occur.

## What the Dashboard Shows

- Distribution of severe incidents across infrastructure environments  
- Classification of environments using NAICS industry codes  
- Concentration of operational risk across sectors  
- Geographic clustering of incidents  
- Organizations operating within high-risk environments  
- Patterns linking infrastructure complexity to isolation risk  

---

## Why This Matters

Modern infrastructure environments are increasingly complex and often include:

- Redundant electrical feeds  
- Backup generation systems  
- Uninterruptible power supply (UPS) systems  
- Multi-layered electrical distribution networks  

While these systems improve reliability, they significantly increase the difficulty of verifying full energy isolation during maintenance.

This dashboard demonstrates how operational data can be used to:

- Identify high-risk environments  
- Surface patterns in incident concentration  
- Support safer and more informed operational decision-making
